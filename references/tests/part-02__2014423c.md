## Part 2 of 2

 The plan clicks into place, cold and sharp as a blade. A living vault. You'd be the courier, she'd be the cargo. It gets you past the gates, past the outer wards, right into the heart of his power. But then what? A crowded room, a dozen eyes, a lord surrounded by guests and guards. You'd be the man who walked in and murdered a noble in his own home. There'd be no escape, no anonymity. The entire might of House Kelk and every allied noble in Parada would be hunting a face they'd all seen.

 Talia's faint smile vanishes, replaced by a flat, unimpressed stare. "You think I intend to be carried into his receiving hall in front of his entire court? No." She shakes her head, her tail giving a sharp flick. "The cargo would be delivered to a secure room; a vault, a study, a counting room. Somewhere private, under guard, but separate from the festivities. That's where the verification happens. That's where he would come."

She leans closer, her voice dropping to a whisper that carries in the silent hallway. "He wouldn't trust a roomful of drunk sycophants with the actual exchange."

**You say**

> You say, "So then it's you and the slime with him. He's checking over payments, documents, "cargo" but unlike the other cargo your restraints would be theatric, the slime "keeping everything safe" would be a knife in the dark waiting at your side to spring 
As a courier would they even let me in? What would I do at that point just wait for you?"


Talia shakes her head, her eyes narrowing as she diagrams the scenario in her mind. "No. A courier doesn't wait with the cargo. The slime would be the guardian; I'd be the item inside the slime. You'd deliver the living vault to the designated secure room, present the manifests and seals to the steward or the lord himself for verification. Once the authenticity is confirmed, you'd be paid and dismissed. The slime would remain, under guard, until the lord is ready to... open it."

She taps a claw against her own forearm. "The slime is the key."

**You say**

> You say, "And that's.. what you want to do? Go in as cargo that's far less restrained that you appear and wait to be checked by the lord?
It honestly sounds like a beautiful plan I just feel nervous letting you take such a dangerous role, you're usually like a shadow, this role paints you front and center, do you feel comfortable with that? I know you literally came up with the plan I just.. wouldn't be there then? I could only provide help through the slime. From outside at that point"


Talia's expression doesn't change, but her ears flatten slightly against her skull. "Comfortable? No. It's the only vector that gets a weapon inside his inner sanctum without triggering every ward and guard between here and his study. And yes, I'd be front and center. That's the point. He needs to see the 'cargo' to verify it."

She pauses, studying your face. "You wouldn't be outside. You'd be gone. A courier's job ends at delivery and payment. You'd walk out the front gate, find a place to wait. The slime is the link."
