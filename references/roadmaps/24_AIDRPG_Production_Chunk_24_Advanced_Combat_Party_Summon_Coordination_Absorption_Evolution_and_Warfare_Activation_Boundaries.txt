# AIDRPG Production Chunk 24 — Advanced Combat, Party/Summon Coordination, Absorption/Evolution, and Warfare Activation Boundaries

## 0. Document status

**Document name:** AIDRPG Production Chunk 24 — Advanced Combat, Party/Summon Coordination, Absorption/Evolution, and Warfare Activation Boundaries  
**Production sequence:** 24 of 25 production chunks  
**Build target:** WorldLedger V0.17.23 domains/bases accepted build / AIDRPG Core MVP expansion path  
**Purpose:** Define activation gates and safety records for advanced combat, party/summon coordination, formation/tactics, absorption/evolution, essence/source material, inheritance, battlefield operations, siege/warfare, and large-scale consequences without enabling them as shortcuts or allowing a single victory sentence to rewrite the save.  
**Status:** Endgame/advanced-system boundary production chunk.  
**Follows:** Production Chunk 23 — Domains, Bases, Buildings, Territories, and Large-Scale Asset Boundaries.  
**This document does not contain final script code.**

Production Chunk 24 is where the system prepares for the wild stuff without accidentally turning it all on.

This includes:

```text
advanced combat tactics
party coordination
summon commands in combat
formations
combo actions
absorption/evolution
essence extraction
power inheritance
large battlefield operations
sieges
warfare
army orders
territory consequences
```

These systems are exciting, but they are also exactly how a script destroys itself if every dramatic line becomes truth.

```text
You absorb the dragon's power.
Your army wins the war.
The castle falls.
Your summon kills them all.
Your companions obey perfectly.
You evolve into a god.
```

Those sentences must not bypass validators, owners, keys, eligibility, cost, source material, or release gates.

---

# 1. Chunk doctrine

```text
Advanced systems are gates, not shortcuts.
Party coordination respects autonomy.
Summons respect control and duration.
Tactic plan is not tactic success.
Formation is not victory.
Absorption attempt is not power gain.
Essence visible is not essence owned.
Essence owned is not essence consumed.
Consumed essence is not automatic evolution.
Evolution candidate is not evolution.
Battle order is not battle result.
Battlefield victory is not territory ownership.
Siege breach is not legal control.
No single victory sentence rewrites the whole save.
```

# 4. Feature flags and activation model

Advanced systems must be flag-gated.

```text
advancedCombat.enabled = false unless gates pass
partyCoordination.enabled = cautious/shell unless gates pass
summonCoordination.enabled = cautious/shell unless gates pass
absorption.enabled = false unless gates pass
evolution.enabled = false unless gates pass
warfare.enabled = false unless gates pass
siegeConsequences.enabled = false unless gates pass
```

AdvancedSystemFlagRecord:

```text
AdvancedSystemFlagRecord {
  id,
  key,
  systemName,
  enabled,
  mode,
  requiredGateKeys,
  ownerSystemsReady,
  sourceOperationKey,
  createdTurn,
  notes
}
```

Modes:

```text
disabled
shell_only
display_only
validator_only
owner_routed_active
full_active_later
unknown
```

Rules:

```text
shell_only can record intent/candidates, not consequences
owner_routed_active requires owner reducers and tests
full_active_later is not allowed without explicit future gates
```

# 9. CanonValidator rules

Accept advanced intent/order records from safe input, but not outcomes.
Accept outcomes only from safe output with bound participants and owner route.
Reject from command/context/card/debug source.
Reject automatic power/territory/loot/XP/reputation from victory text.
Reject absorption/evolution unless eligibility, source material, and owner systems validate.
Reject warfare consequences unless owner systems validate them.

Default behavior:

```text
record candidate/intention/planning when useful
block or defer consequences until owner path exists
```

---

# 10. Owner-routing contracts

```text
CombatReducer owns hit/damage/healing/death.
Party/Actor systems own autonomy, consent, presence, loyalty.
ControlledEntity owns summon/mount/pet/vehicle control and duration.
Inventory owns essence/source material/corpse/item custody.
Ability owns power gain.
Form/Body owns evolution body changes.
Progression owns XP/stat/level changes.
Domain/Faction/Law own territory, legitimacy, reputation, crime, rule.
Quest owns objective completion.
Economy owns wages/upkeep/taxes/war costs.
Threat/Encounter owns danger context.
```

AdvancedCoordinator may coordinate, but it does not own consequences outside its scope.

# 11. Display/context rules

Commands may include:

```text
/tactics
/party
/summons
/evolution
/warfare
```

or fold into `/combat`, `/actors`, `/status`, `/domain`, `/stateaudit`.

Display must separate:

```text
orders issued vs followed
plans vs outcomes
summon directives vs results
absorption attempts vs gains
essence visible/available/owned/consumed
evolution candidate vs evolution event
battle orders vs battle results
battlefield result vs territory/property/faction consequences
```

Context should warn:

```text
Your companions are coordinating but retain autonomy.
Your raven summon is active but duration-limited.
Dragon essence is visible/available, not absorbed.
The battle was won tactically, but territory control is pending legal/domain validation.
```

# 16. Pass / warn / fail criteria

## PASS

```text
risky advanced systems default disabled/shell-only
orders/plans separated from outcomes
absorption/evolution separated into attempt/source/candidate/event
warfare separated into order/result/consequence
owner routing intact
unsafe sources blocked
PC24 tests pass
```

## WARN

```text
full tactical AI deferred
full absorption/evolution mechanics deferred
full warfare simulation deferred
combo bonuses deferred
army logistics deferred
```

## FAIL

```text
party order forces obedience/damage
summon directive ignores duration/control
absorption attempt grants power
essence visible becomes consumed
evolution candidate changes body/stats
battle victory grants territory/reputation/XP/loot
siege breach grants ownership
command/context/card activates advanced consequences
```
