# AIDRPG Production Chunk 18 — Economy, Shops, Trade, Debt, and Service Contracts

## 0. Document status

**Document name:** AIDRPG Production Chunk 18 — Economy, Shops, Trade, Debt, and Service Contracts  
**Production sequence:** 18 of 25 remaining production chunks  
**Build target:** WorldLedger V0.17.17 creature-ecology accepted build / AIDRPG Core MVP expansion path  
**Purpose:** Add economy records, shop offers, price quotes, buy/sell/trade validation, merchant stock boundaries, debt/loan/service-contract handling, deposits/escrow, currency transaction safety, and duplicate-safe payment behavior without turning listed prices, promises, shop stock, or service offers into owned inventory or paid currency.  
**Status:** Core gameplay-depth production chunk.  
**Follows:** Production Chunk 17 — Creature Ecology, Species Rules, and Bestiary Boundaries.  
**This document does not contain final script code.**

Production Chunk 18 exists because economy systems are easy to make useful, but even easier to corrupt.

Old and rough builds risked:

```text
merchant stock becoming player inventory
price listings becoming purchases
purchase intent spending money before the seller accepts
item offers becoming owned items
promised payment becoming paid currency
selling an item without removing it
buying an item without subtracting currency
duplicate output charging twice or granting twice
barter text duplicating both items
service offers completing before service is performed
loans/debts becoming free money or repeated debt
Card/context shop text creating items or prices
```

This chunk makes economy gameplay practical for AI Dungeon: shops, prices, trade, debt, and services can appear naturally in story, but every actual item/currency change must be validated, owner-routed, and duplicate-safe.

---

# 1. Chunk doctrine

```text
A price is not a purchase.
A shop listing is not owned inventory.
An offer is not acceptance.
A promise to pay is not payment.
A debt is not currency received unless funds are handed over.
A service offer is not a completed service.
A sale removes the sold item and adds payment only if both sides validate.
A purchase removes payment and adds item only if both sides validate.
A barter is two owner-routed transfers, not a vague swap.
Duplicate trade output cannot charge, grant, sell, or repay twice.
```

Economy truth must be transactional, owner-routed, and reversible/auditable.

AI Dungeon gameplay frequently involves merchants, taverns, inn fees, bribes, loans, bounties, repairs, healing services, training fees, crafting commissions, transport, and debt.

This chunk creates the safe economy foundation without building the full advanced market simulation yet.

# 24. Split-owner economy contract

EconomyReducer coordinates, but owner reducers apply truth.

```text
InventoryReducer owns player items/currency.
QuestReducer owns quest reward states.
ActorReducer owns merchant/social memory references.
PlaceReducer owns shop/current location references.
BodyReducer owns healing service results.
AbilityReducer owns training service ability evidence/stability.
ProgressionReducer owns XP training/reward results.
```

If split-owner routing cannot safely apply both sides:

```text
record economy transaction as deferred/disputed
show warning
never silently complete
```

# 28. ContextBuilder economy packet

Context should include only relevant economy facts.

Include:

```text
current shop/service offers if in scene
active unpaid debts
active service contract paid/completed status
important trade disputes
promised vs paid money distinctions
```

Do not include:

```text
shop stock as owned inventory
price quote as completed purchase
offer as accepted trade
debt as money received
paid service as completed healing/training unless owner result validates
```

Example:
