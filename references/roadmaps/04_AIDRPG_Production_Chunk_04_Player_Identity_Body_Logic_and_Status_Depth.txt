## Identity model requirements

Player identity must distinguish:

```text
true identity
confirmed name
aliases
origin
world knowledge status
true race/species
lineage
current form
body type
body traits
disguise
illusion
presentation
candidate traits
pending traits
rumored traits
unknown traits
```

True identity cannot be overwritten by current form, disguise, illusion, rumor, NPC quote, dream, temporary wording unless validated, command display, card prose, or context prose.

Current form is the active body presentation or validated body state. It may affect future constraints, but this chunk only prepares/display constraints unless transformation is explicitly validated.

Disguise/illusion are presentation states unless validated as actual body/form changes.

Candidate and pending traits remain unconfirmed.

## Origin and world knowledge model

Fields or equivalents:

```text
origin.status
origin.type
origin.source
worldKnowledgeStatus
worldIntegrationStatus
centralityAssumption
```

Recommended values:

```text
origin.type = unknown | native | outsider | world_emerged | scenario_defined
worldKnowledgeStatus = unknown | limited | local | trained | expert | hidden_lore_unlocked
worldIntegrationStatus = unknown | outsider | tolerated | recognized | entangled | influential
centralityAssumption = none | local_only | scenario_defined
```

Unknown origin does not mean native. Unknown race does not mean human. Limited world knowledge is default unless setup proves otherwise.

## Body resource model

Resources:

```text
hp mp ep
```

Each supports current, max, known, source, lastChangedTurn, notes. Unknown numeric resources must display unknown rather than fake values like HP 100/100.

Rules:

```text
current cannot exceed max when both known
current cannot be below 0 when known
max cannot be below 1 when known
structural clamp is not healing
recovery requires accepted recovery/rest/healing event
```

## Fatigue/exhaustion model

States:

```text
rested winded fatigued weary exhausted collapsed unknown
```

Severities:

```text
none minor moderate major critical unknown
```

Fatigued/worn out does not become exhausted unless strong wording confirms it. Exhausted/can barely stand/drained completely may become exhausted if validated. Collapsed/unable to stand may become collapsed/critical if validated.

## Condition model

Condition examples:

```text
bleeding burned poisoned sick stunned blinded deafened frozen chilled cursed infected exhausted fatigued unconscious restrained
```

ConditionRecord:

```text
id, key, displayName, family, severity, status, sourceOperationKey, sourceEventId, createdTurn, updatedTurn, expiresTurn, location, cause, evidence, notes
```

Statuses:

```text
active worsening improving resolved suppressed pending rejected
```

Condition gain requires direct validated body/status event. Resolution requires recovery/resolution event. Cleanup cannot resolve active conditions.

## Injury model

Injury examples:

```text

```

InjuryRecord:

```text
id, key, displayName, bodyPart, severity, status, bleeding, infectionRisk, sourceOperationKey, sourceEventId, createdTurn, updatedTurn, healedTurn, cause, evidence, notes
```

Threats, attempts, metaphors, dreams, quotes, and card/context/command text do not create injuries.

## Death boundary model

Allowed:

```text
alive unconscious dying dead captured unknown
```

Rules:

```text
alive is not perfect health
unconscious is not dead
dying is not dead
dead requires direct validated death outcome
captured is not dead
unknown means unknown, not dead
```

Reject death from rumor, quote, dream, metaphor, threat, insult, prophecy, card, context, command, or story title text.

## Body trait and form constraint shell

May store/display traits and constraints such as claws, wings, tail, fur, scales, no human hands, cannot speak normally, quadruped movement, large body constraints. Deep mechanics are deferred. Illusions/disguises do not create physical constraints. Candidate traits do not create confirmed constraints.

## BodyReducer behavior

Status gain creates/updates ConditionRecord, sets active/severity, records operation key, marks body_status/context dirty, audits compactly.

Injury gain creates/updates InjuryRecord, body part/severity/bleeding if known, marks body_status/context dirty.

Recovery sets condition/injury improving or resolved and does not delete history immediately.

Resource change applies validated delta/state, clamps safely, records source key.

Death boundary change requires direct validated outcome, durable key, dirty targets, high-severity audit. Unsafe flags cause reject/no-op.
