# AIDRPG Production Chunk 14 — Threat Benchmark, Encounter Logic, and World Scaling Boundaries

## 0. Document status

**Document name:** AIDRPG Production Chunk 14 — Threat Benchmark, Encounter Logic, and World Scaling Boundaries  
**Production sequence:** 14 of 25 remaining production chunks  
**Build target:** WorldLedger V0.17.13 form/race/constraints accepted build / AIDRPG Core MVP expansion path  
**Purpose:** Add threat benchmark records, encounter state, region/creature danger labels, fairness boundaries, no-rubber-band scaling enforcement, encounter proposal safety, and world-scaling display rules without turning every monster mention into combat or scaling the world to the player.  
**Status:** Core systems production chunk.  
**Follows:** Production Chunk 13 — Body Form, Race Logic, Transformation Consequence, and Non-Humanoid Constraints.  
**This document does not contain final script code.**

Production Chunk 14 exists because threat and encounter logic is where an RPG script either starts feeling like a living world or becomes fake difficulty math.

Old and rough builds risked:

```text
all enemies scaling to the player automatically
low-level areas becoming impossible because the player leveled up
high-threat regions being softened because the player is weak
monster references becoming immediate combat
rumors spawning enemies
Story Card creature lore becoming active encounter state
context reminders creating fights
current location danger being overwritten by random creature text
form/race traits being treated as instant power scaling
quest danger becoming completed combat
combat victory generating XP/loot automatically
duplicate encounter outputs creating duplicate enemies
```

This chunk locks the threat/encounter boundary. The goal is not a full creature ecology or encounter generator yet. The goal is to define how threat is measured, how encounter state begins and ends, how the world refuses rubber-band scaling, and how AI Dungeon gameplay receives useful danger context without uncontrolled automation.

---

# 1. Chunk doctrine

```text
Threat is not player-scaled by default.
A wolf remains a wolf unless world facts change it.
A dragon remains dangerous even if the player is low level.
A rat does not become legendary because the player is high level.
A creature mention is not an encounter.
A rumor is not an encounter.
A visible enemy is not combat until engagement validates it.
A dangerous region is not an automatic fight.
Encounter victory is not XP or loot unless separate owner events validate it.
Difficulty labels guide context; they do not mutate state by themselves.
```

Threat should be fair because it is honest, not because it secretly bends around the player.

Production Chunk 13 made body form and non-humanoid constraints meaningful. Threat benchmarks come next because they use:


# 5. Threat and encounter state model

Threat state should be structured but not overactive.

Recommended shape:

```text
threat: {
  benchmarks: {},
  sources: {},
  regionThreats: {},
  creatureThreats: {},
  hazardThreats: {},
  dangerContexts: {},
  fairnessBoundaries: {},
  noRubberBandPolicy: null,
  warnings: [],
  notes: []
}
```

Encounter state should be separate:

```text
encounters: {
  activeEncounterKey: null,
  records: {},
  participants: {},
  triggers: [],
  resolutions: [],
  recentEncounters: [],
  warnings: [],
  notes: []
}
```

Do not hide threat data inside combat logs only. Threat can exist without combat. Encounter can exist before combat. Combat can happen inside an encounter, but they are not the same thing.

# 16. Threat benchmark calculation policy

Threat comparison may use player facts, but only for display.

Allowed inputs:

```text
player level if known
player resource/body state if relevant
active severe conditions
current form constraints
known stable abilities
owned/equipped relevant items as references only
place/region danger label
creature/hazard threat label
quest warning label
```

Allowed outputs:

```text
relative danger label
warning text
context note
ReleaseCheck/audit note
```

Forbidden outputs:

```text
enemy stat changes
region level changes
automatic XP changes
automatic loot changes
automatic combat result changes
automatic quest failure/completion
```

Use labels instead of fake precise math unless formulas are explicit:

```text
safe
manageable
risky
dangerous
deadly
overwhelming
unknown
```

Important example:

```text
A wolf may be moderate objective threat.
If the player is exhausted, bleeding, and in a no-hands form, relative danger may be dangerous.
The wolf did not scale; the player's situation changed.
```

# 17. Encounter activation policy

An encounter can activate when source validates direct scene pressure.

Accepted activation examples:

```text
The wolf steps from the brush and growls at you.
Bandits surround you in the alley.
The bridge cracks beneath your feet.
The dragon lands in the square, scattering the crowd.
Mira draws her blade and blocks your path.
```

Rejected activation examples:

```text
People say wolves haunt the mill.
A book describes dragons.
The quest board warns of bandits.
[AIDRPG] Encounter: wolf active.
Story Card: wolves attack travelers here.
Context: a dragon is nearby.
```

Candidate-only examples:

```text
You hear howling in the distance.
Tracks suggest wolves passed recently.
The air smells of smoke and scales.
```

# 18. Encounter boundaries with other systems

## 18.1 Encounter and combat separation

```text
encounter candidate can exist without combat
active encounter can be social/hazard/creature sighting
combat starts only when direct engagement/attack/threat validates
combat victory can resolve encounter
encounter resolution does not create XP/loot by itself
```

## 18.2 Encounter and quest separation

```text
quest objective mentioning wolves does not spawn wolves
quest warning of dragon does not spawn dragon
encounter with quest target can update quest only through QuestReducer objective validation
encounter resolution does not auto-complete quest unless objective completion validates
```

## 18.3 Encounter and progression separation

```text
encounter activation does not grant XP
encounter survival does not grant XP unless exact XP validates
encounter victory does not grant XP unless exact XP validates
threat tier does not auto-calculate XP
player level does not scale XP table
```

## 18.4 Encounter and inventory separation

```text
encounter resolution does not create loot
creature death does not automatically add loot
loot drop creates available scene item only if InventoryReducer validates it
owned loot requires acquisition event
```

## 18.5 Encounter and form constraints

Form constraints should make encounters feel different.

Examples:

```text
wolf form with no hands in a locked-door encounter -> tool-use warning
winged form in cramped tunnel -> movement/space warning
large beast form in narrow alley -> access/movement warning
tiny form against large predator -> danger warning
water-breathing form in flood hazard -> relative danger may improve
exhausted human body facing wolves -> relative danger worsens
```

Rules:

```text
form constraints affect danger context/relative warnings
form constraints do not auto-win or auto-lose encounters
combat/quest/place owner systems still validate outcomes
```

# 19. Threat CanonValidator expansion

CanonValidator must enforce encounter and threat safety.

## 19.1 Accept threat benchmark when

```text
safe output or structured source directly establishes danger level/fact
source is not command/context/card/audit/debug
threat target is bound
not rumor unless stored as rumored/candidate
not player-scaling formula mutation
```

## 19.2 Accept encounter candidate when

```text
safe output shows signs/tracks/distant sound/possible danger
not direct engagement yet
```

## 19.3 Accept active encounter when

```text
safe output places threat in current scene with direct pressure
participant target bound enough
not rumor/book/card/context/command source
```

## 19.4 Accept encounter resolution when

```text
safe output directly resolves encounter by escape, defeat, negotiation, capture, surrender, or disengagement
encounter target binds to active/candidate encounter
not duplicate
```

## 19.5 Reject encounter when

Any are true:

```text
rumor-only
book/lore-only
quest objective-only
Story Card source
context source
command output
hypothetical
dream/vision only
weak mention with no current scene pressure
```



# 27. ContextBuilder threat/encounter packet

ContextBuilder should include concise danger facts.

Include:

```text
active encounter status
objective threat warning
relative danger label if useful
known escape/avoidance possibility if supported

```

Do not include:

```text
all threat benchmarks every turn
rumors as active encounters

```

Example packet:

```text
Threat: A wolf is actively blocking the path. Objective threat is moderate, but you are exhausted and in wolf form with no human hands, making the situation dangerous. The wolf has not scaled to your level; danger comes from the scene and your condition.
```
