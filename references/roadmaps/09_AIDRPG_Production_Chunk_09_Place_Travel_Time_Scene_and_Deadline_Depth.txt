# AIDRPG Production Chunk 09 — Place, Travel, Time, Scene, and Deadline Depth

## 0. Document status

**Document name:** AIDRPG Production Chunk 09 — Place, Travel, Time, Scene, and Deadline Depth  
**Production sequence:** 09 of 25 remaining production chunks  
**Build target:** WorldLedger V0.17.8 actor/social-depth accepted build / AIDRPG Core MVP expansion path  
**Purpose:** Deepen current location, current scene, travel intent, visible/nearby references, route/interstitial handling, time passage, time claims, deadlines, and age-safety behavior without weak mention overwrites or fake time/age canon.  
**Status:** Core gameplay-depth production chunk.  
**Follows:** Production Chunk 08 — Actor Presence, Social Memory, Witnessing, and Relationship Depth.  
**This document does not contain final script code.**

Production Chunk 09 exists because place and time continuity determine whether AI Dungeon gameplay feels like a real world or a constantly-resetting hallucination.

Old attempts often failed by:

```text
weak place mentions overwriting current location
visible places becoming arrival
travel intent becoming arrival
roads/path/interstitial phrases beating named destinations
context/card/command location text becoming canon
quoted or narrated time skips changing age
long time passage duplicating on output replay
deadlines being forgotten or completed from vague text
scene actors/items not matching current scene after movement
```

This chunk makes location and time persistent, conservative, duplicate-safe, and readable. The goal is not full world simulation yet. The goal is to make current place, current scene, intended travel, known references, time passage, and deadlines safe enough for later world bible, region, faction, encounter, and long-run persistence systems.

---

# 1. Chunk doctrine

```text
Visible is not current location.
Nearby is not arrival.
Travel intent is not arrival.
Road is not destination when a named destination exists.
Route is not scene unless confirmed.
Mentioned place is not current place.
Command location text is not canon.
Context location text is not canon.
Story Card location text is not canon.
Quoted time skip is not time passage.
Dream time skip is not real aging.
Literal time passage is duplicate-sensitive.
Age mutation requires strict policy.
Deadline mention is not completion.
```

Place and time truth must be local, source-aware, duplicate-safe, and conservative.

---

# 2. Why this chunk comes now

Production Chunk 08 made actor presence and scene witnesses safer. Places and time come next because they connect to scene actors, scene items, quest objectives, travel and arrival, deadlines, actor memory location, region pressure, creature encounters, world bible references, CardSync current-location mirrors, context prioritization, and long-run persistence.

If place/time is not safe now, later world systems will misfire. A wolf encounter in the market, a chapel scene overwriting a tavern, or a twenty-year quote aging the player can break the whole game.

# 18. Age policy shell

Age updates are high-risk.

Required policy questions:

```text
is player age known?
is player species/race aging model known?
is time passage literal?
is time passage long enough?
is passage duplicate-safe?
is body/identity lock compatible?
is there a reason to update exact age instead of just recording time passage?
```

Rules:

```text
quoted "twenty years pass" does not age player
dream twenty years pass does not age player
narration practice does not age player
literal exile for twenty years may be accepted time passage
exact player age mutation may remain pending if age model incomplete
```

This chunk may record long literal passage without changing exact age unless age policy is implemented.

# 19. Place CanonValidator expansion

Accept travel intent when safe input/output says the player heads/goes/travels toward a destination but has not arrived. Result: update travelIntent, currentLocation unchanged.

Accept strong arrival when safe output directly confirms arrival/entry/entering/reaching destination, target is bound, and it is not command/context/card source, dream/quote only, hypothetical, or merely visible/nearby. Result: update currentLocation/currentScene and clear/mark matching travelIntent.

Accept scene confirmation when safe output confirms current scene/location details and target matches current place or is strong current-scene phrase. Result: update scene/confirm location without overwriting from weak reference.

Accept weak reference when safe source mentions visible/nearby/known/background place with no direct arrival. Result: reference record only.

---

# 20. Time CanonValidator expansion

Accept time advance when safe output directly narrates literal time passage, not quote/dream/metaphor/hypothetical/planning, not command/context/card, and operation key is unique.

Reject or pending when time wording is quoted, storytelling/narration practice, metaphorical, planning intent, hypothetical, prophecy, card/context/command text, or dream/vision.

Examples to reject or keep pending:

```text
You say, "Twenty years pass."
You feel like years pass.
In a dream, a century passes.
If a week passed, the town would change.
[AIDRPG] Time: three days passed.
```

Exact age update requires a stricter policy. Default safe result for long passage is accepted/pending time advance with age update pending/deferred unless age policy is active.

# 25. Reducer defensive guards

PlaceReducer must reject/no-op if routed result has command/context/card/audit/debug source, visible/nearby as arrival, travel intent as arrival, route phrase overriding named destination, weak mention as current location, dream/quote-only location change, missing place key, or wrong owner reducer.

TimeReducer must reject/no-op if routed result has command/context/card/audit/debug source, quoted time skip, dream time skip, metaphor, hypothetical/planning only, age update without policy, duplicate time advance, missing operation key for repeat-sensitive advance, or wrong owner reducer.

Reducers must not blindly trust upstream.
