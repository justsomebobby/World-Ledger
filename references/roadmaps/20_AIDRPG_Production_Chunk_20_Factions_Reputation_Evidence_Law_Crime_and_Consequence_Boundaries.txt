# AIDRPG Production Chunk 20 — Factions, Reputation Evidence, Law, Crime, and Consequence Boundaries

## 0. Document status

**Document name:** AIDRPG Production Chunk 20 — Factions, Reputation Evidence, Law, Crime, and Consequence Boundaries  
**Production sequence:** 20 of 25 remaining production chunks  
**Build target:** WorldLedger V0.17.19 crafting/materials accepted build / AIDRPG Core MVP expansion path  
**Purpose:** Add faction records, reputation evidence, law/jurisdiction records, crime accusation/evidence/conviction boundaries, public/private consequence routing, bounty/fine/jail shells, and duplicate-safe legal/social consequence handling without private events becoming public reputation or rumor/lore/command text creating legal state.  
**Status:** Core gameplay-depth production chunk.  
**Follows:** Production Chunk 19 — Crafting, Materials, Repair, Enchanting Boundaries, and Recipe Safety.  
**This document does not contain final script code.**

Production Chunk 20 exists because factions, reputation, law, and crime can make AI Dungeon feel reactive and alive, but they can also corrupt the whole game if handled loosely.

Old and rough builds risked:

```text
private actions becoming global reputation
rumors becoming legal convictions
NPC accusations becoming confirmed crimes
Story Card faction lore changing player standing
context summaries creating bounties
witnesses who were not present reporting crimes
quest completion automatically changing faction standing
combat victory creating public fame everywhere
merchant suspicion becoming legal guilt
law text applying outside its jurisdiction
bounty notices becoming paid fines
fine due becoming fine paid
jail threat becoming imprisonment
crime attempt becoming completed crime
stealing visible loot becoming owned item or criminal transfer without inventory validation
duplicate outputs stacking reputation changes and bounties
```

This chunk builds the evidence-first social consequence layer.

The system should support fun consequences:

```text
guards investigate theft
witnesses remember crimes
local factions respect or distrust the player
laws differ by jurisdiction
bounties and fines can exist
public heroic acts may build reputation evidence
criminal acts may create suspicion, reports, charges, and legal pressure
```

But those consequences must be routed through evidence, witnesses, publicness, jurisdiction, and owner systems.

---

# 1. Chunk doctrine

```text
Private action is not public reputation.
Witnessed action is evidence, not conviction.
Accusation is not proof.
Suspicion is not guilt.
Crime attempt is not completed crime.
Law exists within jurisdiction.
A bounty notice is not a paid fine.
A fine due is not a fine paid.
A threat of jail is not imprisonment.
Faction lore is not player standing.
Quest fame is not global reputation unless witnessed/reported/validated.
Commands, cards, context, and lore cannot create legal or reputation canon.
```

Faction and law systems must be evidence-based, scoped, duplicate-safe, and honest.

Production Chunk 19 made crafting/material/economy transactions safe.

Faction/law/reputation comes next because it depends on:

```text

threat/encounter resolution from PC14
world lore/faction lore boundaries from PC15

```

If reputation/law is not designed now, later domain, faction, divine, and endgame systems will repeatedly patch around false public consequences.

# 21. Private/public consequence policy

This policy is central.

Private event examples:

```text
You insult Mira while alone.
You steal from an empty room and no one sees.
You secretly help a prisoner escape.
```

Expected:

```text
actor memory only if actor present/informed
private evidence record if validated
no public/faction/global reputation unless report/discovery occurs
```

Public event examples:

```text
The whole market sees you return the stolen purse.
The city guard watches you stab the merchant.
Old Bren publicly praises you for saving the village.
```

Expected:

```text
public knowledge event may record
faction/reputation evidence may update
legal consequence may begin if jurisdiction/law validates
```

# 22. Law/jurisdiction policy

Law is scoped.

Rules:

```text
current place must bind to jurisdiction for formal legal effects
controlling faction must be known or accepted
wilderness acts may have witness/social consequences without formal law
faction-specific law applies only to faction-controlled spaces or faction members when validated
unknown jurisdiction blocks formal crime status beyond suspected/alleged evidence
```

Example:

```text
You pick a noble's pocket in Market Lane under city guard jurisdiction.
```

Can create:

```text
theft crime event if Inventory/scene validates theft attempt/success
witness report if actor/guard sees
legal status if reported/guard observed
```

But:

```text
private unseen theft does not instantly make city wanted status
```

# 23. Crime and owner-system boundaries

Crime records must not invent owner-system facts.

## 23.1 Theft

Theft depends on Inventory ownership/custody and transfer truth.

```text
item visible -> not theft
attempt to take -> attempted theft only
successful owner-routed item acquisition from another owner -> possible theft evidence
```

## 23.2 Assault/murder

Violence depends on Combat/Body/Actor truth.

```text
attack intent -> attempted assault only
confirmed damage to actor -> assault evidence
confirmed death through death boundary -> murder/homicide evidence if law validates
```

## 23.3 Fraud/debt/default

Fraud/debt depends on Economy/Contract truth.

```text
promise to pay -> not default
missed validated due date/payment refusal -> debt/default evidence if contract exists
```

## 23.4 Trespass

Trespass depends on Place/Law/Access truth.

```text
forbidden area lore -> not trespass until place/access/jurisdiction validates
warning ignored in jurisdiction -> possible trespass evidence
```

# 24. Reputation and quest boundaries

Quest state does not automatically produce faction standing.

Rules:

```text
quest completion can create reputation evidence only if faction/actor/publicness validates
unreported quest completion does not create faction-wide fame

public praise/report can create public knowledge/evidence
```

Example:

```text
Old Bren thanks you privately.
```

Expected:

```text
relationship evidence with Old Bren
possibly local faction evidence only if reported/public/faction-linked
```

Example:

```text
Old Bren praises you before the village council.
```

Expected:

```text

village faction reputation evidence possible
```

# 25. Reputation and combat/encounter boundaries

Combat victory is not automatically fame.

```text
private combat victory -> no public reputation unless witnessed/reported

```

# 26. Faction/law CanonValidator expansion

CanonValidator must enforce social/legal safety.

## 26.1 Accept reputation evidence when

All are true:

```text
safe story output or accepted owner event supports social consequence
faction/actor/public group target is bound
publicness/witness/report scope is known or conservative
not command/context/card/audit/debug source
operation key not duplicate
```

## 26.2 Accept faction standing change when

```text
reputation evidence exists
public/faction knowledge exists or representative actor authority validates it
standing formula/policy supports label change
not duplicate
```

Default safe policy:

```text
record evidence first
standing label changes only when strong/public/faction-scoped evidence supports it
numeric score deferred unless formula authority active
```

## 26.3 Accept crime event when

```text
safe story output or owner-system event supports attempted/committed crime
jurisdiction/law binds or crime remains alleged/suspected without formal consequence
victim/item/combat/economy target is bound enough
not command/context/card source
```

## 26.4 Accept legal consequence when

```text
crime/legal status/evidence supports it
actor/faction/jurisdiction authority is present
consequence type is explicit
not merely threat/rumor
not duplicate
```

## 26.5 Reject social/legal mutation when

Any are true:

```text
private event with no witness/report becomes public
NPC accusation becomes conviction
rumor becomes legal status
Story Card says player is wanted
context says reputation changed
command output says bounty active
law lore applies outside jurisdiction
quest/combat/economy event lacks owner validation
```
