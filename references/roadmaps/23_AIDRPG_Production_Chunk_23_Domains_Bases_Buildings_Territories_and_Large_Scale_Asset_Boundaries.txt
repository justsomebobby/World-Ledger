# AIDRPG Production Chunk 23 — Domains, Bases, Buildings, Territories, and Large-Scale Asset Boundaries

## 0. Document status

**Document name:** AIDRPG Production Chunk 23 — Domains, Bases, Buildings, Territories, and Large-Scale Asset Boundaries  
**Production sequence:** 23 of 25 production chunks  
**Build target:** WorldLedger V0.17.22 controlled-entities accepted build / AIDRPG Core MVP expansion path  
**Purpose:** Secure domains, bases, rooms, buildings, storage, construction, staff, territory claims, legitimacy, taxes, upkeep, defenses, income shells, and large-scale asset state without false ownership, fake kingdom control, automatic income, or command/context/card property mutation.  
**Status:** Large-scale gameplay boundary production chunk.  
**Follows:** Production Chunk 22 — Pets, Mounts, Summons, Vehicles, and Controlled-Entity Boundaries.  
**This document does not contain final script code.**

Production Chunk 23 is fun because it makes the world feel ownable: homes, shops, rooms, bases, forts, towns, castles, farms, ships, labs, temples, territories, and domains can matter.

It is dangerous because AI Dungeon prose often treats access, presence, and ownership loosely.

```text
you see a castle
you enter the manor
you sleep in the inn room
you claim the tower
the villagers call you lord
your base has guards
the shop earns gold
your fortress repels the army
```

None of those can automatically become secure ownership, legal control, income, defense victory, or territory rule.

This chunk separates property from access, control, legitimacy, occupancy, construction, storage, staff, income, and territory.

---

# 1. Chunk doctrine

```text
Seeing a building is not owning it.
Entering a room is not owning it.
Renting a room is not owning the inn.
Using storage is not carried inventory.
Claiming land is not recognized rule.
Recognized rule is not absolute control.
Construction plan is not completed building.
A defense exists as context, not auto-victory.
Income is not currency until collected and validated.
Taxes are not automatic money.
Staff are not puppets.
Territory is not won by a single sentence.
```

Large-scale assets should open gameplay, not silently rewrite the save.

---

# 2. Why this chunk comes now

Production Chunk 22 secured controlled entities and vehicles.

Domains and bases come next because they connect to:

```text
inventory storage and custody
property ownership and access
economy upkeep/income/debt
faction/law legitimacy
actor staff and autonomy
quest objectives and service contracts
travel/current place
threat/encounter danger
warfare/siege consequences later
CardSync summaries
```

If base/domain rules are not secure before advanced combat and warfare, one battle output can accidentally grant castles, taxes, armies, and territory.

# 11. CanonValidator rules

Accept property access when output confirms entry/rent/permission.
Do not treat access as ownership.

Accept ownership only when safe output confirms purchase, legal grant, gift/deed, or strict setup ownership.

Accept claim only as claim unless recognition is explicitly validated.

Accept construction progress only when output confirms progress.
Accept completion only when output confirms completed structure.

Accept storage transfer only through InventoryReducer custody/storage transfer.

Accept income/upkeep only as shell unless Economy validates currency flow.

Reject property/domain mutation from:

```text
command output
context output
Story Card prose
rumor/lore only
claim with no recognition
single victory sentence
hypothetical plan
```

---

# 12. Owner-routing contracts

```text
InventoryReducer owns carried items and storage transfers.
Economy owns payments, rent, taxes, upkeep, wages, income collection.
ActorReducer owns staff presence, autonomy, memory, and relationship.
Faction/Law systems own recognition and legitimacy evidence.
PlaceReducer owns current location and property place references.
QuestReducer owns quest objectives involving construction/property.
Combat/Threat systems own attacks, siege damage, and danger context.
DomainReducer owns property/base/domain records and access/control/legitimacy shells.
```

No domain event may directly add currency, complete quests, kill enemies, or force staff obedience.

# 13. Display/context rules

Commands may include:

```text
/base
/property
/domain
/storage
```

or folded display in `/location`, `/inventory`, `/quests`, and `/stateaudit`.

Display must separate:

```text
visible properties
accessible properties
rented/borrowed spaces
owned property
claimed territory
recognized control
storage contents
construction projects
staff assignments
income streams due/uncollected
upkeep due/unpaid
defenses as context
```

Context should say:

```text
You rent a room at the inn; you do not own the inn.
The tower is claimed by you but not legally recognized.
The base chest stores the iron sword; it is not currently carried.
The shop may generate income, but no coins are collected yet.
The palisade helps defend the camp, but it does not guarantee victory.
```

# 19. Final doctrine

```text
A home, base, domain, or territory is only fun if it is stable.
Stable means ownership, access, control, legitimacy, storage, construction, income, staff, and defense each have their own record and owner path.
No single line of story, context, card text, or victory output is allowed to hand the player a kingdom.
```

When this passes, Production Chunk 24 may safely define advanced combat, party/summon coordination, absorption/evolution, and warfare activation boundaries.
