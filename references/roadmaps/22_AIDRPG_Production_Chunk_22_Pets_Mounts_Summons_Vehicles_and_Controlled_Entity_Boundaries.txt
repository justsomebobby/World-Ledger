# AIDRPG Production Chunk 22 — Pets, Mounts, Summons, Vehicles, and Controlled-Entity Boundaries

## 0. Document status

**Document name:** AIDRPG Production Chunk 22 — Pets, Mounts, Summons, Vehicles, and Controlled-Entity Boundaries  
**Production sequence:** 22 of 25 production chunks  
**Build target:** WorldLedger V0.17.21 romance/companion accepted build / AIDRPG Core MVP expansion path  
**Purpose:** Define and secure pets, mounts, summons, vehicles, controlled entities, cargo, riding, boarding, entity commands, autonomy, duration, despawn, and owner-routed consequences without fake ownership, forced obedience, or state bleed between actor, inventory, travel, combat, party, and vehicle systems.  
**Status:** Advanced gameplay boundary production chunk.  
**Follows:** Production Chunk 21 — Romance, Loyalty, Companions, Party State, and Consent/Boundary Safety.  
**This document does not contain final script code.**

Production Chunk 22 exists because pets, mounts, summons, vehicles, and controlled entities all look deceptively similar in prose.

AI Dungeon may say:

```text
a wolf follows you
you ride the horse
the horse is yours
you summon a raven
the raven obeys
you board the wagon
the cart carries your supplies
the ship takes you north
```

Those are not the same kind of state.

The final script must never again collapse them into one vague “companion” or “inventory” concept.

A pet is not automatically a controlled combat unit.
A mount is not automatically owned.
A summoned creature is not automatically permanent.
A vehicle boarded by the player is not automatically player property.
Cargo is not carried inventory.
A command is not a successful response.
A ride is not arrival.
A summon attempt is not a summon.

---

# 1. Chunk doctrine

```text
A nearby animal is not a pet.
A tame animal is not always owned.
A pet is not a puppet.
A mount is not ownership.
Riding is not travel arrival.
Boarding is not vehicle ownership.
Cargo is not carried inventory.
A summon attempt is not an active summon.
A summoned entity is not permanent unless duration rules validate it.
A command is not obedience.
Obedience is not success.
Control is not ownership.
Presence is not control.
Death/despawn is not loot.
```

Controlled-entity gameplay should be fun because entities matter in the scene.
It must be safe because every entity has its own owner, control state, autonomy, duration, and consequences.

---

# 2. Why this chunk comes now

Production Chunk 21 separated romance, loyalty, companion autonomy, party membership, and consent boundaries.

Pets, mounts, summons, and vehicles come next because they connect to:

```text
actor presence and social memory
party state and companion boundaries
inventory custody and cargo
travel/location/arrival
combat event routing
creature ecology and bestiary rules
form/body constraints
quests and escort/delivery objectives
base/domain assets later
advanced combat activation later
```

If controlled entities are not secured before large-scale assets and advanced combat, later features will duplicate mounts, auto-own vehicles, merge cargo into inventory, and force pets/summons to obey impossible commands.

# 4. Required state model

Controlled entities should live in a dedicated owner bucket or equivalent bridge.

Recommended shape:

```text
controlledEntities: {
  records: {},
  pets: {},
  mounts: {},
  summons: {},
  vehicles: {},
  controlLinks: {},
  commands: {},
  responses: {},
  rideStates: {},
  boardingStates: {},
  cargo: {},
  durations: {},
  despawns: {},
  warnings: [],
  notes: []
}
```

Do not store all of these only in actors, inventory, or party state.

ActorReducer may know a creature/driver/horse exists.
InventoryReducer may know cargo/item custody.
PlaceReducer may know vehicle location.
CombatReducer may know a mount or summon participated.
But ControlledEntity logic owns the control relationship and category distinction.

# 13. CanonValidator rules

Accept pet/tame/bond events only when safe output confirms a relationship or taming result.

Reject pet state when:

```text
animal merely appears nearby
animal follows once without bond
rumor says animal belongs to player
Story Card says pet exists
command/context/cardaudit text says pet exists
```

Accept mount/riding only when output confirms mounting/riding.
Reject ownership unless purchase/gift/ownership validation exists.

Accept summon only when output confirms active summon manifestation.
Reject summon when:

```text
player only attempts to summon
spell fizzles
summon is dreamed/quoted/described in lore
summon is card/context output
```

Accept vehicle boarding when output confirms boarding or being aboard.
Reject ownership unless economy/property/vehicle ownership validation exists.

Accept command intent from player input, but commit outcome only from safe output response.

# 14. Owner-routing contracts

ControlledEntity logic must not steal ownership from other systems.

```text
InventoryReducer owns item/cargo transfer into player inventory.
PlaceReducer owns travel and arrival.
CombatReducer owns attack/hit/damage events.
ActorReducer owns presence/social memory.
Creature ecology owns species facts.
QuestReducer owns objective progress/completion.
Economy owns rental, purchase, fee, service contract, and debt.
Party/companion logic owns party membership.
ControlledEntity owns control relationship, entity category, command/response, ride/board/cargo association, and summon duration.
```

If an owner system is missing, record safe deferred state instead of mutating random fields.

# 16. Context rules

Context should include controlled entities only when relevant.

Safe wording examples:

```text
Your wolf companion is friendly but autonomous; it is not guaranteed to obey every command.
You are riding a borrowed horse; the horse is not confirmed owned.
A raven summon is active until scene end; it is not permanent.
You are aboard a hired wagon; cargo in the wagon is not carried inventory.
```

Forbidden wording:

```text
The horse is yours.
The summon obeys perfectly.
The wagon cargo is in your backpack.
The mount carries you to the city.
```

unless owner state truly confirms those facts.

# 22. Final doctrine

```text
Controlled entities are relationships and access states, not shortcuts.
Pets have autonomy.
Mounts carry bodies but do not imply ownership.
Summons have source and duration.
Vehicles have boarding, operation, cargo, and ownership states.
Every consequence routes through the owner system that actually owns it.
```

When this chunk passes, Production Chunk 23 may safely build domains, bases, buildings, territories, and large-scale assets.
