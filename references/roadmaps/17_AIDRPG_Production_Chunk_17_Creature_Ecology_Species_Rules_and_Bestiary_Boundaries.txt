# AIDRPG Production Chunk 17 — Creature Ecology, Species Rules, and Bestiary Boundaries

## 0. Document status

**Document name:** AIDRPG Production Chunk 17 — Creature Ecology, Species Rules, and Bestiary Boundaries  
**Production sequence:** 17 of 25 remaining production chunks  
**Build target:** WorldLedger V0.17.16 context/card-safety accepted build / AIDRPG Core MVP expansion path  
**Purpose:** Add creature ecology records, species rules, bestiary knowledge, observed signs, habitat logic, behavior boundaries, creature weaknesses/resistances as knowledge-gated facts, loot/body-part boundaries, and creature encounter safety without spawning fights, granting powers, inventing stats, or leaking hidden bestiary secrets.  
**Status:** Core world/gameplay systems production chunk.  
**Follows:** Production Chunk 16 — Context Builder Budget, Memory Priority, and Card Sync Safety.  
**This document does not contain final script code.**

Production Chunk 17 is one of the most important gameplay chunks because it decides whether creatures are just random enemy names or actual living parts of the world.

The final AIDRPG system needs creatures to feel like they have:

```text
species identity
habitats
behaviors
signs/tracks/sounds
territory
threat patterns
body parts
loot/source materials
weaknesses and resistances
knowledge gates
regional presence
social/ecological roles
encounter boundaries
combat boundaries
hidden bestiary facts
```

But it must not repeat the old failure loops:

```text
bestiary lore spawning active encounters
creature card text creating enemies in the scene
rumored weakness becoming confirmed fact
hidden bestiary facts leaking into context
player instantly knowing every creature weakness
species lore granting player powers
creature mention creating loot
creature death automatically giving loot or XP
creature threat scaling to player level
generic animals becoming full actors forever
wrong creature signs creating wrong encounters
cleanup deleting active creature evidence or knowledge
```

This chunk creates the creature ecology and bestiary boundary layer.

It is not a full random encounter generator.

It is not full creature combat AI.

It is not automatic loot-table farming.

It is the reliable system that lets those future systems work without corrupting the ledger.

---

# 1. Chunk doctrine

```text
Creature lore is not an encounter.
Creature sign is not creature presence.
Creature presence is not combat.
Creature defeat is not XP.
Creature corpse is not owned loot.
Creature body part is not harvested item.
Rumored weakness is not confirmed weakness.
Hidden bestiary fact is not player knowledge.
Species trait is not player power.
Habitat supports possibility, not spawning.
Ecology guides the world; it does not bypass event validation.
```

Creatures should feel real because they have rules and context.

They should stay safe because those rules do not mutate unrelated systems without owner validation.

---

# 2. Why this chunk comes now

Production Chunk 15 created lore authority and knowledge boundaries.

Production Chunk 16 made context/card output safe and budgeted.

Creature ecology comes now because it depends on both:

```text
world bible lore authority
player knowledge boundaries
hidden lore filtering
threat benchmarks
encounter activation rules
combat event validation
inventory ownership/custody/loot rules
body/form/race constraints
actor presence and generic group handling
place/region/travel context
quest objective separation
progression XP boundaries
context/card non-canon rules
```

If creature ecology is built before those boundaries, it will become a source of accidental spawns, hidden spoilers, free loot, fake XP, and unstable combat.

# 23. Species rules and player race/form boundary

Creature species rules do not grant player powers.

Examples:

```text
Wolves track by scent.
Dragons breathe fire.
Vampires resist normal weapons.
Slimes split when cut.
Ghouls carry disease.
Harpies can fly.
```

Allowed:

```text
create bestiary/species facts
create player-known or hidden knowledge records
create combat-deferred rule labels
create threat/deadliness context
```

Forbidden by default:

```text
player in wolf form automatically gains perfect scent tracking
player with dragon lineage automatically gains breath weapon
vampire lore changes player death boundary
slime lore changes player body rules
harpy lore grants player flight
```

Player form/race power activation remains owner-routed through PC13/Ability/Combat future rules.

# 24. Creature lore and encounter separation

Creature lore can support encounter plausibility.

It cannot activate encounters.

Examples:

```text
World lore: Wolves live near the Old Mill.
Expected: habitat/species record, no encounter.

Output: You hear wolves howling beyond the trees.
Expected: sign/candidate danger, no combat.

Output: A wolf steps onto the path and growls.
Expected: creature instance + active encounter candidate, still no damage.

Output: The wolf lunges and bites your arm.
Expected: encounter/combat/damage owner-routed through Combat/Body.
```

# 25. Creature loot and inventory separation

Creature loot is possibility, not ownership.

Rules:

```text
loot profile does not create items
creature death does not create owned loot
corpse presence may create scene item/corpse reference if validated

body parts require acquisition validation
loot duplicates require operation keys
```

Examples:

```text
The wolf has valuable pelts. -> lore/loot possibility, no item
The wolf falls dead. -> corpse/death if combat validates, no pelt owned
I skin the wolf and take its pelt. -> possible inventory acquisition only if validated
```

# 29. Creature knowledge discovery flow

Safe knowledge flow:

```text
1. Lore/source creates species fact, rumor, hidden fact, or blocked claim.
2. Player sees sign/creature/behavior or hears/reads information.
3. Observation/discovery record updates CreatureKnowledgeRecord.
4. BestiaryEntryRecord updates visible known/suspected facts.
5. ContextBuilder includes only player-known/relevant/safe creature facts.
```

Do not skip from:

```text
hidden bestiary fact -> normal context
world lore -> player expert knowledge
rumor -> confirmed weakness
```

# 30. Creature CanonValidator expansion

CanonValidator must enforce creature safety.

## 30.1 Accept species/bestiary lore when

```text
source is allowed by LoreAuthority/WorldBible boundaries
claim is creature/species/habitat/behavior/threat/weakness/loot lore
claim does not attempt active encounter/combat/loot mutation
operation key not duplicate
```

Result:

```text
species/bestiary/lore records only
no active encounter or inventory/combat mutation
```

## 30.2 Accept creature sign when

```text
safe output directly shows tracks/sounds/marks/nest/etc.
place/scene target is bound enough
not command/context/card source
```

Result:

```text
CreatureSignRecord and possible observation/candidate danger
no creature presence unless output directly confirms it
```

## 30.3 Accept creature presence when

```text
safe output directly places creature in current/near scene
species/instance target can be bound
not rumor/lore/book/card/context/command source
```

Result:

```text
CreatureInstanceRecord present/nearby/distant according to text
EncounterState may receive candidate/active encounter proposal if pressure exists
no combat damage
```

## 30.4 Accept creature behavior observation when

